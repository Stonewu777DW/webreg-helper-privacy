Last updated: October 28, 2026

WebReg Helper for USC Students ("WebReg Helper") is a free, unofficial Chrome extension made by a USC student. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Southern California. By using it you agree to these terms.

## The extension

WebReg Helper adds tools to USC's WebReg and catalogue pages. It is provided as is, without warranty. Seat counts, requirements and course data come from USC's public pages and may be wrong or out of date. Always check official USC sources and your advisor before registering. The developer is not responsible for missed registrations, seats or requirements.

## Notify Me alerts

Notify Me emails you when a seat opens in a WebReg section you choose. Alerts are best effort: the server checks seats about every 5 minutes, and an alert can be late or missed. Seats are not held for you. To stop email alerts, press **Stop these alerts** in any alert email.

## Text message (SMS) terms

- **Program:** WebReg Helper seat alerts. You get a text when a seat opens in a WebReg section you asked to watch.
- **Opt-in:** you opt in by entering your mobile number and checking the text alert consent box in the Notify Me popup. Consent is not required to use WebReg Helper.
- **Frequency:** one text each time a seat opens in a section you're watching, at most once an hour per section, for up to 120 days per alert. Message frequency varies.
- **Cost:** Msg & data rates may apply.
- **Opt-out:** reply **STOP** to any text to stop all texts. You'll get one confirmation text and no more after that. To opt back in, reply **START**.
- **Help:** reply **HELP** to any text, or email stonewu777@gmail.com.
- **Carriers:** carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
- **Privacy:** your number is used only to send these alerts, and it is never sold or shared for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the [Privacy Policy](https://stonewu777dw.github.io/webreg-helper-privacy/).
